BELT JACKET

L I N E 0 : A R T I S A N A L

Multidimensional Texture

It expresses the rhythm created by the different leather colors (espresso, ochre, etc.) of each vintage belt. The unique texture of each piece comes together in perfect harmony as a single work of art.

Deconstructed Utility

It emphasizes that the belt buckle is not merely a decoration, but a 'functional element' that can adjust the silhouette, as well as Margiela's signature 'avant-garde statement'.

The Artisanal Philosophy

It fully encapsulates Margiela's core value of 'Upcycling' and the brand's deconstructive philosophy of elevating everyday objects into high fashion.
